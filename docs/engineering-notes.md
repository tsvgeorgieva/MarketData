# Engineering notes

## Start here: the specification

**The specification is the main source for the design of this solution:** [issue #18, Spec: VSG market data service](https://github.com/tsvgeorgieva/MarketData/issues/18). These notes do not repeat it. The spec covers:

- **Business decisions**: the problem, the business solution and the assumptions behind it.
- **Technical design and architecture**: the technical solution and the implementation decisions.
- **Story planning**: the user stories that were split into the implementation tickets.
- **Testing decisions**: how the solution is tested.
- **Future improvements**: what was left out of this iteration, on purpose.

## 1. Items not completed due to time constraints

1. **Binance infrastructure client.** It needs to be polished before a production deployment.
2. **Unit tests.** They were generated by AI and have not been reviewed. They need to be structured and reviewed, with AI, under guidance.
3. **Console client.** It was reviewed at a high level only. It needs a detailed review and polish before production.
4. **No automatic reconnect or backfill after a connect.** If the Binance connection drops, collection stops until a restart and the missed minutes stay a gap (see the [README](../README.md#assumptions-departures-and-known-limits)). Other items not built are listed in the spec's [Future improvements](https://github.com/tsvgeorgieva/MarketData/issues/18#future-improvements).
5. **Azure deployment and CI/CD.** The deployment needs a more advanced and detailed design. The current one is only a high-level direction. Nothing is built or tried: no Bicep, no pipelines, no deployment. See [deployment-design.md](deployment-design.md).

1. **Research and planning: `wayfinder`.** One research ticket per open question (XML negotiation, Azure hosting, integration testing), then a grilling session per design ticket in which the agent questions the author until a decision is settled. The input was the assessment PDF, the budget and the earlier decisions. The results are tracked on the map ticket, [#2 Wayfinder: VSG assessment implementation spec](https://github.com/tsvgeorgieva/MarketData/issues/2). Its research and grilling tickets are linked from there.

## 3. How AI output was verified

Each step above was followed by an iteration and a detailed review by the author before the next step started: the research and decisions, the spec, the tickets and each pull request. The exceptions are the unreviewed parts listed in section 1.

## 4. Where the author overruled the AI

Examples where the AI's proposal was rejected or reduced, from the session history, the pull request review comments and the ticket history.

**Specification**

1. **Ingestion that watches itself.** The AI designed a check every 30 seconds that asked Binance which symbols it was sending and reconnected when they did not match. The author limited it to when a symbol is added or removed and moved the loop to future improvements.
2. **Operations beyond the brief.** The AI added a readiness view with connection state, last frame, last Candle and reconnect counts per symbol. The author removed it as scope the assessment does not need.
3. **Redundant response field.** The AI added an `isPartial` flag to the moving average response. The author removed it, because it follows from the data points used and requested.
4. **Structure.** The AI organised the Application layer as use cases and gave the console client its own copy of the response models. The author chose business services, and a client that shares the API's contract project.

**Implementation**

5. **Ingestion structure.** The AI wrote the database writing inside the ingestion service. The author required a separate writer job fed by its own singleton queue, questioned dropping the oldest candle and had the queue sized to survive a 24-hour database outage (PR [#35](https://github.com/tsvgeorgieva/MarketData/pull/35)).
6. **Layering.** The AI placed interfaces next to their callers and made the Binance stream return raw messages. The author required every interface that Infrastructure implements to live in `Domain/Interfaces`, with the stream returning closed Candles only (PR [#35](https://github.com/tsvgeorgieva/MarketData/pull/35)).
7. **Over-built symbol add.** The AI loaded the whole symbol list to check a symbol and count the cap, and handled concurrency. The author asked for direct lookups and ruled concurrency out of scope (PR [#40](https://github.com/tsvgeorgieva/MarketData/pull/40)).
8. **Reconnect.** The author cut automatic reconnect and the backfill after every connect from the build to keep the rest clean in the time available. It is stated as a known limit in the README.
